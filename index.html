Frequently asked questions

Who is Everpath Family for?

Everpath Family is being developed for families who want practical, encouraging ways to build routines, support children's growing independence, and recognize progress.

Is Everpath just a chore chart?

Our vision goes beyond chores. Everpath is designed around routines, age-appropriate responsibilities, encouragement, rewards, and family growth.
